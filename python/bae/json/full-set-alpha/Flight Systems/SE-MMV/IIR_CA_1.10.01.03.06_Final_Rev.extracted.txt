Integrated Implementation Review (IIR)
Control Account 1.10.01.03.06
LS-SPT Prototypes – PSE – Support Equipment Systems Engineering
Executive Summary
This Control Account manages the systems engineering activities supporting Prototype Support Equipment (PSE). It provides the technical foundation for prototype design, integration, verification, and transition to subsequent development phases. The WBS indicates a focused but well-structured systems engineering effort that supports prototype maturation while reducing technical and integration risk. From a logistics and O&S perspective, the account provides a solid foundation for sustainment, but continued focus on logistics integration, maintainability, and lessons learned will be essential for successful transition to production and long-term support.Overall Health: GREENOverall Confidence: HIGH (90%)
Control Account Purpose
Develop and maintain prototype technical requirements.
Establish and manage the prototype technical baseline.
Support architecture and interface definition.
Plan verification and validation activities.
Provided logistic & engineering support during prototype integration and testing.
Maintain configuration control throughout prototype development.
Scope Assessment
Systems engineering scope is appropriately aligned with prototype objectives.
Engineering activities are distinct from hardware development.
Work packages are logically organized and measurable.
Planning packages are limited and appropriate for future effort.
Scope supports downstream design, procurement, and testing.
Technical Baseline Assessment
Requirements traceability supports prototype maturity.
Architecture products provide a stable design framework.
Interface definition reduces integration risk.
Technical reviews should continue throughout prototype development.
Configuration management supports baseline stability.
Included logistics and supportability criteria in technical reviews to influence prototype design.
Verification & Validation
Verification planning begins early in the prototype lifecycle.
Requirements verification is aligned with planned test activities.
Prototype testing will provide valuable design feedback.
Maintain traceability between requirements, verification methods, and results.
Maintain close coordination with logistics, procurement, and sustainment teams to ensure prototype outputs are supportable and ready for transition.
Integration Assessment
Engineering supports prototype integration readiness.
Close coordination with Design & Procurement organizations is evident.
Interface management reduces integration issues.
Engineering & Logistic participation during integration testing is recommended.
Risk Assessment
Prototype requirement changes remain the primary technical risk.
Interface modifications could affect test readiness.
Configuration drift should be minimized through disciplined change control.
Overall execution risk is assessed as LOW–MODERATE.
Identified and tracked supply chain risk in the risk register
EVMS / IBR Assessment
Clearly defined engineering scope.
Logical work package structure.
Appropriate planning package usage.
Technical activities support measurable progress.
No significant structural deficiencies identified.
Report logistics readiness metrics as part of EVMS/IBR reviews.
Recommended Actions
Maintain rigorous requirements traceability.
Conduct recurring technical baseline reviews.
Coordinate closely with prototype integration and test teams.
Convert planning packages before execution.
Capture lessons learned to support future production engineering.
Engage logistics SMEs early to ensure prototype scope addresses maintainability, provisioning, and training needs.
Overall Reviewer Conclusion
This Control Account reflects a disciplined prototype systems engineering approach consistent with expectations for a major Department of Defense acquisition program. The engineering scope provides a solid technical foundation for prototype development, verification, and integration. Risks identified are typical of prototype engineering and are manageable through effective configuration management, interface control, and technical oversight. Overall assessment: IIR Ready with HIGH confidence. Early logistics SME engagement, sustainment data capture, and transition planning are critical. These additions will ensure logistics is not just considered but actively drives design for supportability and readiness for production.
