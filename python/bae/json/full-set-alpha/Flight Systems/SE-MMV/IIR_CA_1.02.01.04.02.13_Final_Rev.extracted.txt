Control Account 1.02.01.04.02.13
LS-SPT Increment 1 Support Equipment Development – Design & Procurement
Executive Summary
This Control Account encompasses the design, development, procurement, fabrication, and delivery of Increment 1 Support Equipment. It demonstrates a mature hardware development approach that transitions engineering outputs into producible support equipment through disciplined procurement and manufacturing activities. Early alignment of support equipment specifications with O&S requirements (e.g., maintainability, reliability, provisioning, and training) will reduce downstream sustainment risk.Overall Health: GREENOverall Confidence: HIGH (88%)

Detailed support equipment design
Procurement planning
Long-lead material acquisition
Vendor coordination
Fabrication support
Hardware integration support
Delivery of operational support equipment
Configuration-controlled hardware releases
Scope Assessment
The WBS decomposition demonstrates a logical progression through the hardware realization lifecycle.
Design activities logically precede procurement.
Procurement scope is separated from engineering effort.
Hardware development activities are clearly identifiable.
Configuration-controlled hardware is incorporated into planning.
Work packages are executable and planning packages are appropriately limited.
Continued synchronizing procurement with engineering releases and logistic deliverables
Design Maturity Assessment
Engineering products support procurement readiness. Should require logistic sign off at design reviews
Design activities are sufficiently decomposed.
Configuration management supports hardware baseline.
Monitor engineering changes, supplier availability, and component obsolescence.
Procurement Assessment
Procurement follows engineering maturity.
Supplier activities are planned.
Material acquisition supports production schedules.
Maintain configuration control throughout procurement.
Ensure lifecycle support in all procurement packages.
Overall Procurement Risk: LOW TO MODERATE
Manufacturing Readiness
Manufacturing receives mature engineering outputs.

Configuration Management
Maintain controlled drawing releases.
Use procurement package version control.
Implement engineering changes through formal configuration management.
Maintain hardware serialization where applicable.
Integration Assessment
Supports hardware availability for integration.
Provides supplier support.
Ensures configuration-controlled deliveries.
Maintain schedule alignment with integration teams.
EVMS / IBR Assessment
Clearly defined scope.
Executable work packages.
Proper separation of engineering and procurement.
Logical technical flow.
No significant structural deficiencies identified.
Recommended Actions
Monitor logistic & engineering maturity before procurement authorization.
Conduct recurring supplier readiness reviews.
Closely monitor long-lead material acquisition.
Maintain strict configuration management.
Convert remaining planning packages before execution.
Embed logistics supportability, maintainability, and provisioning as key performance parameters in all support equipment design and procurement activities
Actively manage DMSMS and supply chain risks.
Overall Reviewer Conclusion
This control account exhibits the characteristics expected of a mature Design & Procurement control account supporting a major DoD acquisition program. Execution risks are manageable through disciplined supplier oversight, configuration management, and engineering change control. The control account is mature and supports logistics and O&S objectives. Continued focus on logistics deliverables, configuration management, and supplier readiness will ensure support equipment is fully supportable and cost-effective throughout its lifecycle.
