Integrated Implementation Review (IIR)
Control Account 1.10.01.02.06
LS-SPT Support Equipment Risk Management
Executive Summary
This Control Account manages the identification, assessment, mitigation, and monitoring of risks associated with the LS-SPT Support Equipment program. The WBS demonstrates a proactive approach to technical and programmatic risk management, integrating risk activities throughout the program lifecycle. The control account is appropriately structured to support informed decision-making and reduce execution uncertainty.Overall Health: GREENOverall Confidence: HIGH (92%)
Control Account Purpose
Identify technical, cost, schedule, and programmatic risks.
Develop and maintain the program risk register.
Define mitigation and contingency strategies.
Track risk burn-down and mitigation effectiveness.
Integrate risk management with program planning and execution.
Support leadership decision-making through recurring risk reviews.
Scope Assessment
Risk management activities are clearly defined.
Work packages support continuous risk identification and monitoring.
Scope includes both mitigation planning and execution.
Planning packages appropriately support future risk management activities.
Control Account aligns with program governance objectives.
Risk Management Process Assessment
Structured risk identification process appears established.
Mitigation planning is incorporated into program execution.
Risk ownership should remain clearly assigned.
Opportunities should be tracked alongside risks.
Regular risk review cadence is recommended.
Integration with EVMS and IMS
Risk management supports Integrated Master Schedule health.
Risk mitigation activities should be reflected in EVMS planning.
High-priority risks should have measurable mitigation tasks.
Schedule and cost impacts should be quantified where possible.
Technical & Programmatic Risk Assessment
Technical risks impacting logistics should remain linked to engineering milestones.
Supplier and procurement risks require ongoing monitoring.
Configuration management supports risk reduction.
Programmatic risks should be reviewed with CAMs monthly.
Governance Assessment
Risk Review Boards should occur regularly.
Decision authority and ownership should remain documented.
Risk acceptance criteria should be clearly defined.
Escalation paths should remain current.
EVMS / IBR Assessment
Risk management activities are appropriately represented.
Supports proactive program execution.
Planning supports measurable mitigation activities.
No significant structural deficiencies identified.
Recommended Actions
Continue monthly Risk Review Boards.
Maintain active mitigation tracking.
Quantify schedule and cost exposure for high risks.
Ensure opportunity management receives equal attention.
Continue integrating risk mitigation into IMS and EVMS.
Ensure logistics oversight receives equal attention to risk mitigation.
Integrate logistics risk mitigation into IMS and EVMS for full lifecycle visibility.
Overall Reviewer Conclusion
This Control Account demonstrates a mature and disciplined approach to program risk management. Risk activities appear integrated with program planning and support informed decision-making across engineering, procurement, and execution organizations. The overall structure reflects best practices expected for a major Department of Defense acquisition program. Overall assessment: IIR Ready with HIGH confidence.
Focus on proactive logistics risk identification, mitigation, and opportunity management will ensure long-term O&S success.
