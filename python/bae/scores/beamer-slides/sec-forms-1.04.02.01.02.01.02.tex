\textbf{Scope and Deliverables Definition \(c_1\).}
The scope is explicitly defined as work that does not fit elsewhere, which is the opposite of clear definition. There are no identified deliverables, boundaries, or completion criteria. This represents a critical gap in scope definition.\par\vspace{0.6em}
\textbf{Schedule Planning and IMS-Loading Readiness \(c_2\).}
There is a complete absence of planning detail. The description provides no basis for defining activities, estimating durations, identifying dependencies, or applying any form of quantity-based data. The readiness for IMS loading is non-existent.\par\vspace{0.6em}
\textbf{Cross-Product/Cross-Function Integration \(c_3\).}
By its nature as a collection of miscellaneous integrated assembly designs, this WBS element should be rich with cross-product interfaces. However, none are identified or documented, indicating a major deficiency in integration planning.\par\vspace{0.6em}
